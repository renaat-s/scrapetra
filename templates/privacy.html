Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026

1. Introduction

ScrapeTra ("we", "our", "us") is operated by Nanoclone Systems. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit www.scrapetra.com (the "Service").

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: API key usage, email addresses submitted through campaigns.
  • Payment Information: Processed securely through Stripe and PayPal. We do not store card details.
  • Usage Data: IP addresses, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on the Service.
  • Scraped Data: Business contact information gathered from publicly available sources.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain the Service.
  • To process payments and deliver lead data.
  • To send transactional emails (pitch emails, CSV delivery).
  • To improve and optimise the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share information with:

  • Payment Processors: Stripe and PayPal for transaction processing.
  • Email Providers: For delivering transactional and outreach emails.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law.

5. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the Service. Campaign data and leads are stored in our database until deleted.

6. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your personal data.
  • Correct inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your data.
  • Object to processing of your data.

7. Cookies

The Service uses minimal cookies for session management. We do not use tracking cookies or third-party analytics cookies.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including HTTPS encryption, secure API key authentication, and token-gated file downloads.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

10. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at info@scrapetra.com.
